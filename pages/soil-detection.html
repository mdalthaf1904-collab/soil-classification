75 Micron Decision

If percentage passing the 75 micron sieve is 50% or less, the soil is treated as coarse grained and continues to Grain Size Distribution. If it is greater than 50%, the soil is treated as fine grained and proceeds to Atterberg Limits. Sedimentation analysis may be performed if detailed particle-size distribution below 75 micron is required. This software continues with Atterberg Limits for soil classification.
